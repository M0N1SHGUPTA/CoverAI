ApplyAI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 20, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 20, 2026

ApplyAI ("the Extension") is a Chrome browser extension that helps job seekers generate tailored cover letters and cold emails. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. We believe in minimal data collection — we only process what's necessary to generate your message, and we don't store your personal data on our servers.

TL;DR

Your resume stays on your device. Job descriptions are processed in memory and never saved. We don't track you, sell your data, or store your personal information on our servers.

1. Information We Collect

The Extension collects and processes the following data only when you explicitly trigger it:

  • Resume Text — text you paste or import from a PDF in the "My Resume" tab.
  • Job Listing Content — job description text scraped from the active tab when you click "Scan Page".
  • Page URL & Title — the URL and title of the job listing page you scan.
  • Generation Preferences — your selected tone (professional, enthusiastic, etc.) and message type (cover letter or cold email).

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Data Where It Lives Duration
Resume text Local Only   Chrome's local storage on your device Until you clear it or uninstall
Job description In Transit   Sent to our server for processing Not stored — processed in memory only
PDF resume file In Transit   Uploaded for text extraction Deleted immediately after extraction
Generated messages Local Only   Displayed in the extension popup Until the popup closes
Browsing history Not Collected —
Personal identity info Not Collected —

3. How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used exclusively to generate job application messages:

  • Your resume text and job listing content are sent to our backend server over HTTPS.
  • Our server forwards this data to the Groq API (powered by Meta's LLaMA models) to generate a tailored cover letter or cold email.
  • The generated message is sent back to the Extension and displayed in the popup.
  • If you upload a PDF, it is sent to our server for text extraction using pdfplumber (an open-source Python library). The file is deleted immediately after extraction.

We do not:

  • Store your resume, job descriptions, or generated messages on our servers.
  • Use your data for advertising, profiling, or analytics beyond anonymous usage counts.
  • Sell, share, or rent your data to any third party.
  • Train AI models on your data.

4. Third-Party Services

The Extension relies on the following third-party service for AI generation:

Groq API

We use Groq's inference API to run Meta's LLaMA language models. Your resume excerpt and job description are sent to Groq's servers for processing. Groq's privacy policy is available at groq.com/privacy-policy. Per Groq's policy, data sent through their API is not used to train models.

5. Server-Side Analytics

Our backend collects minimal, anonymous analytics to monitor service health:

  • Total number of API calls (generate and upload endpoints).
  • Approximate unique user count based on IP addresses.
  • Timestamps of recent requests.

This data is held in memory only and resets every time the server restarts. IP addresses are used solely for rate limiting and anonymous counting — they are never logged to disk, shared with third parties, or linked to any personal information.

6. Permissions Explained

Permission Why We Need It
activeTab To read the job listing content from the tab you're currently viewing, only when you click "Scan Page".
scripting To inject a content script that extracts the job description text from the page.
storage To save your resume text locally on your device so you don't have to re-enter it every time.

The Extension does not request access to browsing history, bookmarks, downloads, or any data beyond the active tab you explicitly choose to scan.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • All communication between the Extension and our server uses HTTPS encryption.
  • Our server enforces rate limiting to prevent abuse.
  • Resume text and job data are processed in memory and never written to disk on our servers.
  • PDF uploads are written to a temporary file that is automatically deleted after text extraction.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • You can delete your saved resume at any time by clearing the text in the "My Resume" tab and saving.
  • You can uninstall the Extension at any time, which removes all locally stored data.
  • You can choose not to scan a page — the Extension never reads any page content without your explicit action.
  • No account or sign-up is required to use the Extension.

9. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
